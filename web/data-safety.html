Google Play Data Safety Disclosure · Effective 2026

Data Collection Declaration

⬒ No Data Collected

LUNE does not collect, transmit, or share any user data. All information entered into the application remains exclusively on your device. This declaration applies to all data types defined in Google Play's Data Safety requirements.

Medical Disclaimer

LUNE is designed for personal health tracking and educational purposes only. This application does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Period predictions are statistical estimates and should not be relied upon for medical decisions, contraception, or family planning without consulting qualified healthcare professionals.

Health Connect Integration

LUNE integrates with Google Health Connect and Apple Health for optional data synchronization:

◆ Health Connect permissions are requested only when users choose to enable synchronization
◆ Health data access is limited to menstrual cycle information (read and write)
◆ All synchronization occurs locally on the device through system APIs
◆ No health data from Health Connect is transmitted to external servers
◆ Users can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time through system settings

Contacts Permission Policy Compliance

LUNE does not request READ_CONTACTS permission or access to user contact information. The application does not require contact access for any functionality. This complies with Google Play's 2026 Contacts Permissions policy requiring use of Android Contact Picker for contact access.

Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

LUNE does not integrate third-party libraries or SDKs that collect, transmit, or process user data. The application uses only system frameworks and open-source libraries that operate locally without external communication:

◆ Android Jetpack Compose for user interface
◆ Kotlin Coroutines for asynchronous operations
◆ DataStore for local encrypted storage
◆ Health Connect SDK for optional health data synchronization (no external transmission)

No analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or data collection frameworks are present in the application.

Data Export and Portability

Users have complete control over their data with robust export capabilities:

◆ JSON backup export for complete data portability in machine-readable format
◆ PDF report generation for medical consultation and record-keeping
◆ Image-based report sharing for easy distribution to healthcare providers
◆ All exports are generated locally without server processing

Regulatory Compliance

LUNE's privacy-by-design architecture ensures compliance with major data protection regulations:

◆ GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation): No personal data processing on external systems
◆ CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act): No sale of personal information
◆ HIPAA principles: Health information remains under user control on their device
◆ Google Play Data Safety: Full transparency in data handling disclosure
