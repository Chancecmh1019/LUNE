Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

LUNE was built on a foundational principle that your personal health information belongs exclusively to you. This privacy policy outlines our approach to data handling, which prioritizes local storage, user control, and complete transparency about how your information is managed.

Information We Do Not Collect

LUNE does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information on external servers. The application operates entirely on your device. Specifically, we do not collect:

◆ Menstrual cycle data, including period start and end dates, flow intensity, or cycle predictions
◆ Health symptoms, mood tracking, medication records, or clinical notes entered by users
◆ Personal identifiers such as name, email address, phone number, or user account credentials
◆ Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
◆ Usage analytics, behavioral data, or interaction patterns within the application
◆ Advertising identifiers or data used for targeted advertising purposes

How Your Data is Stored

All data entered into LUNE is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage mechanisms provided by your operating system. Your menstrual records, symptom logs, and personal notes never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export or back up this information through features you control.

The application uses Android's DataStore and iOS's local storage frameworks, which employ system-level encryption to protect your data at rest. Access to this data is restricted to the LUNE application and requires your device authentication credentials.

Health Data Integration

LUNE offers optional integration with Apple Health and Google Health Connect. When you choose to enable this integration:

◆ Data synchronization occurs entirely on your device through system-provided health APIs
◆ No health information is transmitted to LUNE servers or third-party services
◆ You control which specific health data types LUNE can access through system permission settings
◆ You may revoke health data access at any time through your device's system settings

Health data permissions are managed exclusively by Apple Health or Google Health Connect. LUNE cannot access health information without your explicit authorization through these platforms.

Data Export and Backup

LUNE provides tools for you to export your data in industry-standard formats. When you use these features:

◆ JSON backup files are created locally and saved to your device storage or shared through your chosen application
◆ PDF reports are generated on-device and shared directly through system sharing mechanisms
◆ No export data passes through LUNE servers or external processing services
◆ You control where exported data is sent through your device's native sharing interface

Notification Services

LUNE offers local notification reminders for upcoming periods and ovulation windows. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using system notification frameworks. No notification data, scheduling information, or reminder content is transmitted to external servers or notification services.

Third-Party Services

LUNE does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The application does not communicate with external servers for any purpose. No software development kits from advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers are embedded in the application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You have complete control over data retention through:

◆ Individual record deletion within the application interface
◆ Complete data clearing through the settings menu, which removes all menstrual records and resets the application
◆ Application uninstallation, which removes all locally stored data according to system policies

When you delete data within LUNE or uninstall the application, no residual data remains on external servers because no data was transmitted to external servers at any point.

Data Security

LUNE employs multiple security measures to protect your information:

◆ Local data encryption using operating system cryptographic frameworks
◆ Application-level access controls that prevent unauthorized data access
◆ No network communication that could expose data to interception or unauthorized access
◆ Regular security audits and updates to address potential vulnerabilities

Medical Disclaimer

LUNE is designed for personal health tracking and educational purposes. The application does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Period predictions and cycle insights are based on statistical algorithms and should not be relied upon for medical decisions, contraception, or family planning without consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

Individuals undergoing medical treatment, managing diagnosed conditions, or experiencing irregular menstrual patterns should consult healthcare providers for appropriate medical guidance. LUNE is a tracking tool that complements but does not replace professional medical care.

Children's Privacy

LUNE is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used this application, please contact us immediately so we can provide guidance on data removal.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or application features. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of LUNE after policy updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and LUNE does not collect or process personal information on external systems, you maintain complete control over your information at all times. You have the inherent right to:

◆ Access all data stored within the application at any time
◆ Export your data in portable formats
◆ Delete any or all data stored within the application
◆ Use the application without creating an account or providing personal identification

Compliance Information

LUNE complies with applicable data protection regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Data Safety requirements. The application's privacy-by-design architecture eliminates many regulatory compliance concerns by not collecting personal data in the first place.

For Google Play users: This application declares no data collection in the Data Safety section because no user data is transmitted from the device. All data handling occurs locally on your device.
